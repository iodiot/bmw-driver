# BMW M5 F10 — side reconstruction, iteration 22

The side geometry was rebuilt against the side, top, front and rear projections in the BMW M5 February 2012 press kit. This is a reconstruction from the dimension drawing and photographs, not BMW's original CAD. The drawing establishes dimensions and outlines; panel curvature and stamped detail require photographic interpretation.

Changed: four solid door shells and real panel gaps; continuous belt and lower-door sections; front/rear arch folds and complete wheelhouses; sculpted rockers; window outlines and their rising lower edge; A/B/C pillars and roof rails; a crowned roof with curved plan boundaries; curved front/rear glazing; relocated body-colour handles with recesses; mirrors; front side vents; hood/cowl and trunk/glass transitions.

Verified: 4910 mm length, 1892 mm body width, 1451 mm height, 2964 mm wheelbase, 1627/1582 mm track, 2119 mm mirror width and 836/1110 mm overhangs. New door, roof, hood, trunk and glass skins are closed meshes. Paired side panels are mirrored. Tyre envelopes fit the opening contours at 0 and ±25° steering. Four wheel rigs and six opening mechanisms remain functional. Dark steel rotors and deep red calipers were retained.

606 meshes · 633,868 vertices · 1,250,618 triangles · 26.018 MiB GLB. No additional Glow Rush/SF3D generation was run for this iteration; the generation ledger remains at nine raw runs.
